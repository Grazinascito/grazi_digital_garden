# Studylogs

Daily capture when you study. One entry per study day — synthesis title, date filename, `study_day` for 100-day challenges.

- **Filename:** `YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- **Title (H1):** what you studied today, in your own words
- **Sunday ritual:** extract 1–2 ideas that still matter 48h later into notes, [[essays/index|essays]], or [[how to/index|how to]]
